About Us

Where technology meets opportunity.

A global university empowering the next generation of leaders through skills based, technology driven education.

Who we are

An education built for the way the world works

American Open University exists to prepare students for the future of work. Every program is skills-based and delivered with technology, so what you learn maps to the competencies employers are actually hiring for.

We cultivate digital literacy, ethical awareness and innovative problem-solving, bridging academic knowledge with practical application. Our flexible model makes an industry-recognised education affordable and possible alongside work and family life.

Our purpose

Why we exist

Mission

To leverage cutting-edge technology to provide affordable, flexible and industry-recognised education that prepares students for high-demand jobs and equips them to adapt to the ever-evolving technological landscape.

Vision

To provide a skill-based and technology-driven education that prepares students for the future of work, equipping them with the competencies to thrive in the global interconnected economy and empowering them to make a positive global impact.

The difference

What sets us apart

Our approach is career driven and career focused: equipping learners with practical skills, leveraging technology to transform learning, and preparing graduates to thrive in a rapidly evolving digital workplace.

Our educational model

The EDGE Framework

At the heart of American Open University is the EDGE Instructional Leadership Framework, a learner centric model that guides the design of every program we offer. Unlike traditional approaches, EDGE centers the learner's journey, emphasizing individual growth, empowerment and career advancement across four interconnected pillars.

  1. E

    Educate

    The what

    We ensure you acquire a solid foundation of knowledge and practical skills aligned with the evolving needs of the workplace.

  2. D

    Develop

    The who

    We shape you into a well-rounded professional with the skills and competencies demanded by your chosen career path.

  3. G

    Grow

    The where

    We bridge theory and practice by connecting your learning to real-world industries, internships and job functions.

  4. E

    Empower

    The how

    We equip you with the tools, mentorship, professional networks and certifications to achieve long-term success.
