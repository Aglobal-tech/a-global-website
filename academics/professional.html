Academics · Professional

The Edge Institute for professionals and leaders.

Welcome to The Edge Institute at American Open University Nigeria. Our programs are offered across two distinct pathways designed to meet your specific career goals.

Pathways

Two ways to advance your career

Professional Certificates

6-month, intensive, hands-on programs

Designed to build job-ready technical and business-launching skills. Each program features a dual-credential outcome: alongside your Professional Certificate, the curriculum is designed to prepare you for and help you earn an additional, globally recognized industry certification from leaders like Google, Cisco, or Adobe.

Executive Diplomas

6-month, strategic programs for experienced leaders

Designed to cultivate the management, governance, and strategic foresight needed to lead teams, departments, and organizations. These programs are reserved for experienced professionals and leaders ready to move beyond technical execution into strategic decision-making.

Professional certificates

Available professional certificate programs

Professional Certificate in IT Support Specialist

Your launchpad for a career in technology. Powered by the Google IT Support Professional Certificate curriculum, it provides a rigorous grounding in troubleshooting, networking, operating systems, and security, so you graduate ready to diagnose and resolve technical issues in an IT specialist role.

Professional Certificate in Cybersecurity

An intensive program that transforms graduates into combat-ready defenders prepared for AI-automated cyberattacks. Integrating the Cisco Network Security curriculum with our AI Defense context, you master network infrastructure, active defense, and threat hunting, learning to think like an attacker to build a defender's mindset.

Professional Certificate in Data Analytics & Business Intelligence

Become a data-driven decision-maker. Combining Cisco's data science curriculum with hands-on Microsoft Power BI mastery, you move beyond spreadsheets to extract insights that drive corporate strategy, mastering Python, SQL, and interactive dashboards.

Professional Certificate in AI-Powered Software Development

A project-based immersion into software development: master a full technology stack while leveraging AI to accelerate coding. Whether building tools for established organizations or launching a startup, you graduate with a portfolio of real, working applications as proof of competence.

Professional Certificate in Advertising, Marketing, and Technology

A creative bootcamp for a career in modern advertising. Master the intersection of creative design, digital marketing strategy, and ad-tech platforms, guided by industry leaders and working on real marketing campaigns.

Professional Certificate in Learning Development

A hands-on program that teaches you to build digital education. Master instructional design tools and techniques to create engaging content, then deploy and manage courses inside enterprise-grade Learning Management Systems.

Executive diplomas

Executive diploma programs

Executive Diploma in Human Capital Development

This diploma is for strategic architects of company culture and performance. You will move beyond HR administration to master strategic talent management, organizational design, succession planning, and using data to drive people-related business decisions.

Executive Diploma in Information Technology Management

This program is designed to bridge the critical gap between technical expertise and executive leadership. You will master the business of IT, focusing on governance, project management, budgeting, and aligning technology strategy with enterprise-level business goals.

Executive Diploma in Strategic AI Development

This program is for leaders, not coders. It demystifies AI and provides a strategic framework for identifying high-ROI opportunities, building a business case, and managing AI projects. You will learn how to lead your organization through the AI revolution, focusing on practical deployment and ethical considerations.

Executive Diploma in Public Design and Development

This is Nigeria's premier practical policy program, designed to bridge the gap between political theory and the real-world mechanics of effective governance. You will learn how to analyze, design, fund, and implement policies that drive national progress.

Executive Diploma in Strategic Education Design

This unique diploma addresses the most pressing challenge for Nigerian higher education: how to build a financially sustainable, technologically advanced, and globally competitive institution. It is a program designed by education leaders, for education leaders.

Executive Diploma in Aviation Administration

This highly specialized diploma addresses the unique challenges of the aviation industry. You will master the complex interplay of airport and airline operations, international safety regulations, passenger experience, and financial management in this high-stakes sector.

Admissions

Admission requirements

Professional certificate pathway

This pathway is designed for individuals building foundational career skills. Ideal candidates include:

  • Recent graduates: Bachelor's degree (or equivalent) with little or no professional experience.
  • Early-career professionals: 1 to 5 years of work experience, seeking to specialize or gain technical skills.
  • Career changers: motivated professionals from any field looking to pivot into a new industry or role.

Executive diploma pathway

This pathway is reserved for experienced professionals and leaders. There are two routes for admission:

  • Standard Entry: Applicants must possess a Bachelor's degree (or equivalent) and a minimum of five (5) years of relevant professional experience.
  • Executive Assessment Route: In exceptional cases, business leaders or senior professionals without a Bachelor's degree may be considered. Candidates must demonstrate a significant track record of seven to ten (7-10) years of progressive industry experience, including managerial or leadership responsibilities.

Delivery

Program structure and delivery options

All Edge Institute programs, regardless of the pathway, consist of a carefully curated curriculum of three courses that must be completed in a sequential order. To accommodate the diverse needs of our students and corporate partners, we offer three distinct delivery modes:

Structured Cohort

For learners who thrive on a schedule and peer interaction. Delivered in three distinct 7-week sections alongside a cohort of fellow students.

Self-Paced Independent Study

Maximum flexibility for the disciplined, self-motivated learner. Move through the three sequential courses at your own pace.

Workplace Sponsored Intensive

An in-person, cohort-based model for corporate clients: three intensive one-week courses, all completed within an 18-month period.
