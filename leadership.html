Leadership

The people building A-Global.

Our leadership brings together academic rigour, industry experience, and public-service commitment to guide a university designed for the future of work.

Meet our Global Advisory Council

Our Global Advisory Council comprises a distinguished assembly of international educational leaders, seasoned industry experts, influential political figures, and dynamic entrepreneurs from across Africa, the United States, the Middle East, and Asia, who collectively provide strategic guidance, foster global partnerships, and champion innovative solutions to real-world challenges, ensuring our students are equipped to lead and innovate on a global stage.

Dr. Lenora Mosby

Workforce Development Expert & Executive Coach

Dr. Lenora Mosby

Dr. Lenora Mosby is a human resource development expert with more than fifteen years of progressive experience in performance improvement, learning and development, organisational leadership, change management, process improvement, and project management.

As an authentic thought leader and workforce development specialist, she advises institutions on building learning cultures that prepare professionals for the future of work.

Prof. John Ifediora

Emeritus Professor of Economics, University of Wisconsin

Prof. John Ifediora

Professor John Ifediora is an Emeritus Professor of Economics at the University of Wisconsin, where he taught for over 25 years. He is currently a Visiting Professor at New York University and the University System of Maryland, as well as American University in the Washington DC Area. He has provided consulting services to various state governments in the United States. Notably, he advised the Wisconsin Department of Revenue on its Tax Incidence Study, served as a special consultant to the Federal Reserve Bank of Chicago, and provided economic impact analysis to the Illinois State Senate on the state lottery's effects on the economy of Chicago.

Professor Ifediora holds a Ph.D. in Economics from the University of Wisconsin-Madison, a Ph.D. in Economics from the University of Illinois at Chicago, and a Juris Doctor (J.D.) from the University of Wisconsin-Madison. He also obtained an M.A. from the University of Chicago, an M.St. from the University of Oxford, and an M.S. from the University of Cambridge. He completed his undergraduate studies at Dakota Wesleyan University and pursued post-graduate studies at the London School of Economics. Additionally, he serves as a member of the Board of Trustees of Dakota Wesleyan University.

Dr. Susan N. Kahn

Director of Assessment, Rose-Hulman Institute of Technology

Dr. Susan N. Kahn

Dr. Susan N. Kahn serves as Director of Assessment at Rose-Hulman Institute of Technology. Dr. Kahn has held several key roles advancing institutional effectiveness in higher education, such as coordinating the Graduate and Professional Education track of the Assessment Institute and serving as officer for the Knowledge Development Task Force with the Association for the Assessment of Learning in Higher Education.

She has collaborated on the Grand Challenges in Assessment Project and serves on the advisory board for Leading Improvements in Higher Education, a service of the Assessment Institute hosted by IUPUI. Dr. Kahn consults with state and national government agencies, including the United States Department of Education and the National Science Foundation, on P20W educational policymaking and programming.

Senator (Dr.) Fatai Buhari

Senator of the Federal Republic of Nigeria, Oyo North Senatorial District

Senator (Dr.) Fatai Buhari

Senator (Dr.) Fatai Buhari is a distinguished legislator representing Oyo North Senatorial District in the Nigerian Senate. He currently serves as Chairman of the Senate Committee on ICT and Cybercrime and brings decades of public-service and policy experience to the council.

His work in technology governance, legislative oversight, and constituency development strengthens A-Global's commitment to producing ethically grounded, digitally fluent graduates.

Dr. Suzanne Scott

Senior Implementation Strategist, Edward Jones Investment

Dr. Suzanne Scott

Dr. Suzanne Scott has worked in the high-tech industry as a Learning Architect, Technical Training Manager, Global Training Manager, and Senior Instructional Designer at various Fortune 500 companies such as Apple Computer, Cisco Systems, Hewlett-Packard, and Agilent Technologies. She is currently a Senior Implementation Strategist at Edward Jones Investment.

She has a Bachelor of Science degree in Business Administration from California Coast University, a Master of Arts degree in Education from San Jose State University, and a Doctorate in Educational Leadership from Lindenwood University.

Dr. Mahmoud Elrefaey

Chief Operating Officer, Al Sharq Healthcare (UAE)

Dr. Mahmoud Elrefaey

Dr. Mahmoud Elrefaey is a senior healthcare executive and the current Chief Operating Officer of Al Sharq Healthcare, a leading healthcare provider in the UAE. In this role, he is responsible for the overall operations of the organization, including clinical and non-clinical services. He has a proven track record in leading and advising on operational delivery, strategic planning, and performance management to ensure high standards of patient services.

Dr. Elrefaey has held key positions in healthcare organizations such as the World Health Organization, Stanford Healthcare Consultancy, NMC Healthcare, and Dr. Sulaiman Al Habib Medical Group. In these roles, he was responsible for a wide range of activities, including strategic planning, operations management, quality improvement, and revenue cycle management. He holds a DrPH in Global Health from Indiana University Richard M. Fairbanks School of Public Health and several advanced and executive qualifications.

Dr. Sally Nkem Adukwu-Bolujoko

Chairman of the Board, University of Port Harcourt, Nigeria

Dr. Sally Nkem Adukwu-Bolujoko

Dr. Sally Nkem Adukwu-Bolujoko is the Chairman of the Board, University of Port Harcourt, Nigeria. She is a renowned Nigerian scholar, educationist, and entrepreneur who has significantly contributed to industrial relations, education administration, and management consulting. With an extensive educational background and multiple leadership roles, including President of the Nigerian Institute of Management, Dr. Bolujoko has excelled in various fields.

She has actively participated in politics and community service, serving on various government committees and advocating for change. Dr. Bolujoko is a respected author and presenter, and her dedication to education, management, and community development has earned her numerous fellowships and accolades, solidifying her position as a respected figure in West Africa.

El Hadj Lehbib Mohamedou

Diplomat, Mission of Mauritania to the United Nations

El Hadj Lehbib Mohamedou

El Hadj Lehbib Mohamedou is a diplomat in the Mission of Mauritania to the United Nations in New York and a former Advisor to the UN President of the General Assembly, where he served as Adviser in charge of Budget, Legal Affairs, and the Reform of the United Nations. He serves with his country's Mission as Elections Officer and covered other questions such as the cooperation between the G5 Sahel and the United Nations; currently, he oversees the 1st Committee of the UN General Assembly, which deals with Disarmament and International Security.

El Hadj holds a bachelor's degree in international relations from the University of Nouakchott and a diploma from the National School of Administration of Mauritania, diplomatic section. He is currently obtaining a Public Leadership Credential from the Harvard Kennedy School of Government and a master's degree in International Problems and Conflict Resolution from Fairleigh Dickinson University.
