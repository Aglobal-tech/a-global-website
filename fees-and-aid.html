Frequently Asked Questions

Your session fee is all-inclusive: tuition for a full-time course load, access to the learning management system, digital library and courseware, laboratory and virtual lab access, examinations and assessments, academic advising and student support, and the industry certification vouchers bundled with your program. There are no hidden charges added later in the session.

An academic session is a full year of study made up of two semesters. Full-time session fees are billed once per session and may be spread over the term through an approved payment plan.

Eligible students can spread tuition and fees over the first eight weeks of the term in three instalments, apply for merit scholarships, use employer sponsorship, or apply for approved student loan schemes. Our financial aid advisors will help you choose the right combination.

The payment plan allows you to spread your tuition and fees over the first eight weeks of the term in three instalments, making the initial financial commitment more manageable.

Indicate your interest during your application or contact the financial aid office. You will be asked for supporting documents such as academic transcripts or proof of income, and an advisor will guide you through merit scholarship and approved student loan options.

Payments are made through the secure channels listed on your student invoice: bank transfer to the official university account or online card payment inside the student portal. Always keep your payment reference and upload the receipt to your portal record.
