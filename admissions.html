A partnership in your professional journey

Our admissions
philosophy

We do not treat admissions as a gatekeeping barrier. Instead, our evaluation process identifies determined problem solvers who are eager to build demonstrable professional capabilities.

American Open University is designed for focused individuals ready to take their next big step. Our programs are hands-on, rigorous, and highly specialized, created for learners who have a clear direction and are seeking to accelerate their careers, pivot into a new industry, or launch a new venture.

We believe that modern education must harmonize with the realities of busy working adults and forward-thinking youth. We acknowledge your prior education, vocational credentials, and real-world experience, pairing you with curriculum tailored for contemporary industry standards.

"We see ourselves as your strategic partner in achieving your professional goals. Our admissions process is designed to identify applicants who demonstrate this clarity of purpose and are prepared for the intensity of our programs."

American Open University Nigeria

Whether you are applying for undergraduate study or executive postgraduate certification, you will experience a transparent, supportive review conducted by our academic team.

Admission Requirements

01

All applicants must have a minimum of five (5) credits in SSCE/GCE/NECO O’ level examinations, including English Language, Mathematics and the core subject(s), obtained in no more than two sittings.

02

For those transitioning, a certificate of the previously completed degree must be presented.

03

Applicants awaiting examination results are not eligible to apply.

04

Applicants are required to be computer literate to enable them to effectively navigate our ICT-enabled blended learning model.
